Introduction

Welcome to Gujarat Govt Jobs Hub ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services ("Service").

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register for an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Mobile number, used for account matching and contact purposes only
  • Full name and selected exam/post information
  • Account preferences and subscription information

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. The website does not receive or store your plaintext password.

Usage and Technical Data

Depending on the features you use, the Service may collect:

  • Pages visited, timestamps, navigation events, quiz activity and mock-test activity
  • Browser, operating-system, device and screen information
  • Referral and campaign information provided by analytics services
  • Authenticated activity identifiers used for security and account auditing

Google Analytics is used on parts of the Service for aggregate usage measurement. Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database are used for account and application data. Approved subscription receipts may be sent through EmailJS when that feature is enabled.

Payment Information

For premium access, the Service may store the selected plan, amount, order ID and UPI transaction/UTR reference that you submit for administrator verification. Payment credentials such as card numbers, UPI PINs or banking passwords are not requested by the website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To manage accounts, email verification and customer support
  • To process and review premium subscription requests
  • To send technical notices, updates, security alerts, support messages and approved receipts
  • To detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud and technical issues
  • To analyze usage trends and improve educational content
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to operate the Service, including:

  • Firebase / Google: Authentication and Realtime Database storage.
  • Google Analytics: Aggregate website analytics on pages where analytics is enabled.
  • EmailJS: Optional subscription receipt delivery when the receipt-email feature is used or automatically triggered after approved premium activation.
  • Legal Requirements: Disclosure when required by applicable law or a valid request from a public authority.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical controls appropriate to a static web application backed by Firebase, including:

  • Firebase Authentication with email verification
  • Firebase Realtime Database security rules and administrator-only subscription authority
  • HTTPS connections for the public site and Firebase services
  • Regular repository and security-rule validation

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information. Contact us using the address below for account-data requests.

Data Retention

Account and subscription information is retained while needed to provide the Service and for legitimate security, support, accounting or legal purposes. Stale unverified accounts may be removed automatically after the configured verification period.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page.
